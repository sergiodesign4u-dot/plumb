The domain half of the sources is missing for three types, and they carry the MVP. None of the fifteen competitors shows a product surface without an account, and we do not log in, so for T1, T2 and T3, which hold 21 of the 30 MVP nodes, there is no competitor page of that type to open. The named substitute is public documentation of the real product, opened and dated in session, plus barriers from the As-Is journey. For those three types the column "where we are better" holds the barrier a block works on rather than a comparison, because there is nobody to compare against on the same surface.

Not one MVP page is indexed. The card is public but noindex, and the registry, sources, sign-in and system pages are private or transactional. That is why the public landing is in the round for one node: the SEO layer belongs to this stage and would otherwise have no subject.

T1, the read surface: sources and what they gave

SourceKindThe one thing it settled
PowerMetrics metric overviewDomain, documentationTheir metric page keeps the details in an About tab that hides and shows, and opens on charts. That single structural difference is the largest one between their page and our card
dbt data health signalsDomain, documentationThe published criteria show a stale upstream source and a missing description producing the same yellow. Our refusal of any aggregate stops being a preference and becomes evidenced
Zendesk StatusCraft, outside the categoryThe condition stated in a full sentence before any detail, with the last-update time beside it
Enode asset detailCraft, outside the categoryLabel and value rows are the cheapest readable form for provenance, and they collapse to one column at 360
Resend metricsCraft, counter-referenceFreshness in a page footer and the definition behind a tooltip: design principles 1 and 3 broken in one well-made screen

What all five have in common is the gap. Not one puts a definition in words on the reading surface, and not one puts a person's name on the claim. The competitor research reached that conclusion from the outside; the bank now confirms it at the level of page composition.

One competitor motion recorded rather than discovered later: dbt ships data health tiles as an embeddable iFrame for a BI dashboard, which is movement towards the place of reading, our strategic dimension. What travels in that tile is the aggregate signal, not the definition, the attribution or a route to a person.
