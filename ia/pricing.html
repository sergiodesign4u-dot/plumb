The one page in this product that argues with itself

The business model is still a hypothesis in the rules file and in the research, and this is the page that would have to state it as a fact. So the node specifies the structure and names the two things that are not decided, rather than inventing numbers to fill a card.

01

Seven blocks, and one of them exists because no source has it

The comparison that decided block 2

Secoda's pricing page has three tiers and not one price anywhere on it. Core, Premium, Enterprise, each with "Learn more". dbt's has four tiers with prices in the open, and the price is in the meta description.

We take dbt's version outright. For a product selling defensibility, a price you have to ask for is off-brand in a way that is hard to argue out of: the whole claim is that a person should be able to check a thing without asking anybody's permission.

Block 3 exists because no source has it. Every pricing page in the bank says what a plan includes. None says what it will never charge for. Ours does, and it is the business hypothesis stated as a promise: readers are free and the metric count is not capped, because charging per reader kills the growth mechanism and charging per metric penalises the exact behaviour activation is defined as.

Block 4 is one axis instead of six groups. Secoda's comparison runs six feature groups and dozens of rows across three tiers. Ours has the one axis that matters: what a person can do without paying. Read a card, open its source layer, send it on. That is the whole free tier, and it is also the product's argument.

02

The two things this node does not decide

OpenWhy it stays open
The number on the cardQuestion 1 in the research. The market gives no reader-pricing norm to inherit: Secoda gives unlimited viewers on every tier, Cube charges $20 per viewer per month, Looker makes Viewer a separate licence, PowerMetrics caps metrics at 50 and 200
Whether seats are the unit at allA live risk, found on the live page. dbt meters "5,000 queried metrics per month" and "20,000 queried metrics per month". A queried metric per month is a billable unit in this market, and it is the unit we intend to leave free
Why neither is invented to make the page look finished

A pricing page written before the pricing decision becomes the place the decision was accidentally made. The structure is specified, the numbers are not, and question 2 in the research now has a competitor's metered unit standing in front of it.
