One hop, in words, and the discipline is knowing where to stop

The depth half of design principle 4. One line of provenance is on the card already; this is the rest, and almost everything a data catalog would put here is deliberately absent. A reader about to be quoted needs to know whether the number is defensible, not to audit a pipeline.

Type: section Scope: MVP Opens from node 2.1 Source: ia/docs/pages/source-layer.md
01

Where it sits, and why it is not a modal

Not a page. A section of node 2.1, opened from block 6 of the card. Addressable: /m/<stable-id>#source, with any sent-link parameters preserved, so the back button works and the state can be linked to directly.

Not a modal. Design principle 4 forbids a modal as the only route to the source, and the reason is practical rather than stylistic: a modal has no URL, so it cannot be sent, cannot be returned to, and disappears on a refresh.

A hole repaired upwards rather than patched here

Node 2.1 said the card carries one H1 and no H2. Opening this section adds exactly one H2, because it genuinely is a section. Node 2.1 has been amended to say so, in both its source and its page. Two documents disagreeing about heading structure is precisely what the two-layer rule exists to catch.

One hop, and the definition of a hop is the whole node. A hop is from the number to the thing that produced it. It is not from the number to everything that produced that thing. Where the boundary sits is decided here rather than left to whoever draws the wireframe.

Each refusal names who does it, because each one is something a competitor does well.

The pattern in that table is worth naming

Everything refused here is something a data catalog does well, for a data person, in a place they went to on purpose. We are not building a smaller catalog. We are answering one question in the place the number is being read.

States, and the one where this matters most

Open with restricted visibilityThe table or view row is withheld with a stated reason, not silently dropped. Everything else stays
LoadingNot a state. These six rows are metadata we already hold, so they render with the card. Only the value is ever queried at read time
The permission note, and it is the same open question as node 2.10

Secoda restricts what a viewer sees inside lineage by their access, recorded in competitors.md as a live constraint. Our default is to show the table or view name, because a reader who cannot name it cannot ask a specific question, and asking a specific person a specific question is job R1 in full. A workspace that restricts it gets the fourth state above. Whether that default survives contact with a real data lead is open research.

Emotional support: none. All four mechanisms in the emotional and social table sit on the card, the send action, the definition editor and the registry. Recorded so the check is visible.

SEO

Part of node 2.1 and therefore noindex. No canonical, no structured data, no SEO text. One thing this node does owe the URL layer: the open state is addressable, so it has to be a real URL change rather than a script-only state. A crawler will not index it, but a person will paste it, and a link to a provenance view that opens the card closed is a broken promise made by our own product.
