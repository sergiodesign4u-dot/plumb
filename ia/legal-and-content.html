The last-updated date is a design principle, not a convention

Principle 5: every claim carries a name and a time. A legal page is nothing but claims, so a legal page with no date has the same defect as a number with no timestamp, on the one surface where we are asking to be believed about how we handle somebody's data. Both the domain source and the craft source put it under the title, so taking it is agreement rather than borrowing.

H2s written as questions. The live privacy notice writes its sections as "What personal data does Fivetran collect and why?", "Who does Fivetran share my personal data with?", "How does Fivetran keep my personal data secure?" Taken: it is how the reader asks it, it is how a search asks it, and it costs nothing.

The finding this type produced, and it is about a link

dbt's own footer privacy link leaves dbt's own domain. getdbt.com/cloud/privacy-policy redirects to fivetran.com/legal/privacy after the merger. Recorded as a finding rather than a criticism, because it is a real structural risk for anybody: a legal surface on somebody else's domain is one whose wording, canonical and date you do not control. For us the privacy page is a selling argument rather than boilerplate, so it stays on our domain with the rest of the structural SEO layer.

02

The four pages, and the one that is the exception

NodeWhat it is
6.4 DocumentationOne page, not a documentation site. dbt's documentation is a subdomain with its own information architecture, and that is its own IA project. Ours answers two questions: how a definition is written so somebody else can read it, and what the states on a card mean. It is the only page in cluster 6 that renders the state vocabulary from node 0.4, which makes 0.4 its source rather than prose written twice
6.5 Privacy policyThe one legal page that is also an argument. Its content is the table from node 6.2. The row that matters is the absence: a privacy policy that can say we hold no copy of your rows and nothing about who read a number is making the product's case in the document a reviewer trusts least to be making one
6.6 Terms of serviceStandard US B2B terms on the shell, with the date. One structural note: the prior-agreement clause already used at node 1.1 exists because in this market the contract is usually signed before anybody logs in, and the two places have to agree
6.7 Support and contactThe exception: no rail and no prose. A route to a person, what to expect, and the security-report route node 6.2 also names
6.7 is the route out that node 1.3 does not yet have

The sign-in failure "this domain is not set up with Plumb" currently carries an instruction and no link, because this page is ПОТІМ. When 6.7 exists, 1.3 links to it, and the dependency is recorded here so it is not forgotten in the direction that matters.

Refused: a call to action inside a legal document. One craft source puts "Get started" on its terms page. A legal document with a conversion button reads as an ad on the one page where a person is looking for the absence of tricks.

03

SEO block, shared by the map

A. Per page, and only the pattern is fixed: title is the document name plus the product name, description is one sentence saying what the document governs. index, follow on all four, because a legal page that cannot be found before a call is a legal page that generates one.

B. One H1 per page, the document's name. H2s as questions wherever the content allows.

C. Only 6.4 gets prose at this stage, because the other three are legal text that counsel writes and this stage may not draft:

A definition in Plumb is a sentence, not a formula. It says what the metric counts, on what, and over what period, in words somebody outside the data team can check. The query underneath it is what makes it executable, and the reader never sees it. When the definition changes, everybody holding a link to that number is told what it used to say.

6.5, 6.6 and the cookie document carry a marker instead of copy: their text is a legal deliverable, and inventing it here would produce something that reads like a policy and binds nobody.

D. None on 6.5 and 6.6: there is no schema type for a policy that adds anything a crawler needs. 6.4 may carry FAQPage if its content settles into questions, and 6.7 carries ContactPoint inside the Organization already declared at node 6.2.

E. One H1 per page, LCP is the title block and it is text. Crawlable rail links between the documents, which is also the internal linking this stage owes them. Canonical per page, no hreflang. The last-updated date is text, never an image. Prose with no scripts, which makes these the cheapest pages in the product to make fast.
