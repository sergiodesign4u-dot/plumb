02

Roles, refusals, and one rule taken as a rule

There is no reader role at all

Secoda's pricing page lists viewers, editors and admins, with viewers unlimited on every tier. Ours is narrower: a reader has no account, so they cannot be a member of anything. The roles in this workspace are the ones that write, and that is a direct consequence of the card being readable at a permanent URL with no account.

Two controls were promised by a refusal and existed nowhere, found at stage 04

A blocking message has to name an action that exists. The wireframes rendered both refusals and both pointed at nothing: "make somebody else an administrator first" when no screen could change a role, and "change the number of seats on plan and seats" when node 5.2 had no seat control. Repaired here rather than in the wireframe, because drawing the missing control on a screen would have hidden the hole in this node. The role becomes a control in the member row, the seat count becomes a control on 5.2 beside the counter it already carries, and neither is new surface.

The rule this leaves behind is worth more than the two controls: an inline refusal is honest only when the remedy it names is reachable from the screen the person is on, or one named route away. A refusal pointing at nothing moves the failure from the product to the person.

One craft source shows an error toast: you cannot remove the last admin. We take the rule and refuse the toast. A workspace with no administrator is unrecoverable, so this is inline and blocking at the point of the action, per node 0.3: what belongs to the thing on screen stays on screen.
