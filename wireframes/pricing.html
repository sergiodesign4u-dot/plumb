What Plumb costs, and what it never charges for

Seats for the people who write definitions. Everyone who reads a number is free.

Plumb charges for the people who define metrics, and for nobody else. An analyst who connects a source, writes what a metric means and puts their name on it holds a seat. Everyone who opens a number card, reads its definition, checks where the figure came from and passes it on does so without an account and without a licence.

What is never charged for

Never charged for

  • Readers. Any number of them, on any plan.
  • The number of metrics. There is no cap and no tier that raises one.
  • The number of times a card is opened. Reading is not metered.

That is a decision about how this product grows rather than a promotion. A number is worth more the more people can see where it came from, so charging the people who look at it would be charging for the thing that makes it useful. The number of metrics in a workspace is not capped for the same reason: a registry that costs more as it fills up is a registry that stays empty.

Questions about billing and about what we store

Does Plumb store my data?

No. Plumb keeps definitions, owners and connection settings. The value is queried from your source at the moment somebody reads it and no copy of your rows is kept. The detail is on trust and security.

What counts as a seat?

A person who connects a source, writes a definition or puts their name on one. Opening a card, reading its definition, checking where the figure came from and passing it on is not a seat and never becomes one.

Can I pay by invoice?

On the Enterprise plan, yes. On Team, payment is by card.

Sign in to start a workspace

Sign-in is through your identity provider. By signing in you agree to the Terms of service, and what we store is set out in the Privacy policy.
