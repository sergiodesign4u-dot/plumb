Support and contact

How do you reach a person?

Write to support@plumb.app, or use the form on this page. Both arrive in the same place.

What happens after you write?

A person answers on working days, and the reply carries their name and the date it was written. There is no queue number and nothing to track.

Message sent

Your message was sent. The reply goes to the address you gave, from a person, with their name on it.

Nothing else is needed from you. If it is urgent, writing again to support@plumb.app adds to the same thread.

Where do you report a security issue?

Write to security@plumb.app. That route is always open and does not go through the form.
