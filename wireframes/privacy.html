Privacy policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

What does Plumb store about your data?

Definitions, owners and connection settings. No copy of your data rows. A number is queried from your source when somebody reads it and is not kept afterwards.

What does Plumb store about the people who read a number?

Nothing that identifies you. A card is opened at a permanent link with no account. We count how often a metric is opened, and that count names nobody.

What does Plumb hold, and what does it never hold?

We hold

  • Metric definitions, in words
  • Owners, and the date on each claim
  • Connection settings, with secrets write-only
  • The link a card was sent as

We do not hold

  • Any copy of your data rows
  • Values as history. There is one last-run timestamp per metric, and it is overwritten
  • Any credential in readable form, including on our own screens
  • Anything that identifies who opened a card. No cookie, no device identifier, no per-reader log
