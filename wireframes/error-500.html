Node 7.2, MVP. Three blocks in the node's order: what broke, what is safe to assume, retry. The middle block appears in none of the competitor sources and follows from the product: an error page in a trust product has to say whether anything it showed can still be believed, and here nothing was shown, because we never got the number. There is no stale value behind the error for the reason the architecture gives, that we store no copy. The separation that matters is in the wording: a 500 is our failure, while "source is down" is a fact about the customer's warehouse and belongs on the card as node 2.4. The two must never share a phrase, or a reader learns to read our outage as their data being broken. The retry returns to the same address, which is why it is not labelled "home". Not visible on screen: noindex and a real HTTP 500. Interface wording is draft, stage 05 owns it.
