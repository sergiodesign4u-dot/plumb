Node 7.1, MVP, and it is not an edge case: the reader's only entrance is a link, so a broken or expired one is where they meet us. Four blocks in the node's order, one centred column at 360 and at width. There is deliberately no call to action in the signed-out rendering, because the honest act is to ask whoever sent the link, and a button that goes nowhere useful is the wall this node refuses. The tombstone carries three fields only, the name it had, its owner and the date it was removed: no definition, no value, no history. Refused by name at the node and absent here: "contact the owner of the site that linked you", which hands the problem to the one person who cannot solve it; "Go to Homepage", because the reader has no home; and a sitemap footer of twenty-five links, which is a shrug rather than a route. Two decisions are not visible on screen: noindex with follow, and a real HTTP 404, since a soft 404 returning 200 is the version of this page that gets indexed.
